04 / HOW WE HIRE

Screened on the work. Not the keywords.

Every candidate who reaches you has been tested on the job you're hiring for — by people who have done it.

01Day 0 · 30-minute call

The brief

We learn your client mix, software stack, work pattern and team culture — the things a job description never says.

  • Client sectors & engagement types
  • Tally, Zoho, SAP or CaseWare? Compliance tools in use
  • CTC band, location and joining window
02Day 1–2 · Technical screen

The screen

Role-specific assessments built with practising CAs and CSs. Case-based, timed, and reviewed by a human.

  • Audit: sampling, Ind AS adjustments, SA reporting
  • Tax & GST: computations, reconciliations, notice drafts
  • CS: board resolutions, filings timeline, LODR scenarios
03Within 48 hours

The shortlist

Three to five profiles, each with a screen score, a written assessment note and verified ICAI / ICSI membership details.

  • Membership & articleship verification
  • Notice period and counter-offer risk flagged
  • Reference checks before offer
04Offer to day 90

The close & cover

We run interviews, offers and resignation follow-through, then stay close for the first 90 days. If a hire leaves, we replace them at no extra fee.

  • Interview scheduling & feedback loops
  • Offer negotiation and joining follow-up
  • 90-day free replacement
